What is HawBudget?

HawBudget is a smart budgeting app that helps you review your monthly income, debt-to-income ratio, spending, and subscriptions in one place.

You can connect your bank account or manually upload a CSV file to review your financial activity.

Features

💰 View monthly income

📊 Review your DTI

💸 See your spending view

🔁 Track subscriptions

⏰ Set subscription cancellation reminders for premium users

🏦 Connect your bank or upload a CSV file manually

✅ Reviewing monthly income
✅ Understanding spending habits
✅ Checking your DTI
✅ Tracking subscriptions
✅ Uploading financial data manually with CSV

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

HawBudget is a personal finance application designed to help users review income, spending, DTI, subscriptions, and budgeting information.

Users may connect a bank account through Plaid or manually upload a CSV file. Bank login credentials are handled by Plaid Link and are not directly collected or stored by HawBudget.

Data Retention & Disposal Policy

HawBudget retains user data only as long as necessary to provide app functionality, support account features, and meet legal or operational requirements.

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data.
