Chat N' Play

Play together. Right inside Messages.

Chat N' Play brings multiplayer games directly into iMessage, letting you challenge friends and family without leaving your conversation.

About Chat N' Play

Chat N' Play is an iMessage gaming experience designed for playing games with friends and family directly through Apple Messages.

Start a game from your conversation, invite another player, and compete without needing to leave Messages.

Games

  • Spade House – Multiplayer Spades
  • Play directly inside iMessage
  • Challenge friends and family
  • Computer players can fill available seats
  • More games and features may be added over time
