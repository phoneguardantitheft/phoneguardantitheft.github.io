Privacy Policy

Chat N' Play

Effective: August 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

Hawthornes Computer Repair LLC respects your privacy. Chat N' Play is designed to provide games that can be played through Apple's Messages platform without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information to us.

Information We Collect

Chat N' Play does not collect, store, or sell personal information from its users.

We do not require users to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, account credentials, contacts, precise location, or other personal information to use Chat N' Play.

Messages and Gameplay

Chat N' Play operates as an iMessage app and allows users to initiate and participate in games through Apple's Messages platform.

We do not operate a separate messaging service and do not collect or store the contents of your personal iMessage conversations.

Advertising

Chat N' Play does not currently display third-party advertising or use third-party advertising networks to collect information from users.

Children's Privacy

Chat N' Play does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because Chat N' Play does not require user accounts or collect personal information, we do not maintain personal profiles of children using the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the features or privacy practices of Chat N' Play change. Any updates will be posted on this page.
